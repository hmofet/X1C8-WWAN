Install WWAN antennas + cellular modem

1Prep

Manual p. 69–70

Gather parts and tools

  • WWAN antenna kit (it comes with its cables, including the P-sensor cable) and your cellular modem card.
  • Small Phillips driver for the M1.6 / M2 screws, plus a plastic pry tool. general
  • A tray or muffin tin: the screws are different sizes, so keep them sorted by step. general
Check first. Confirm the modem is one Lenovo supports in this machine. The BIOS can reject unsupported cards. Also check whether antenna cables are already installed in your unit before buying more.

Remove external devices and the SIM

Unplug the charger, dock, external display and any microSD card. If a nano-SIM is in the tray, eject it with a pin now.

Disable Fast Startup in Windows

  • Control Panel → view by Large icons → Power Options → Choose what the power buttons do.
  • Click Change settings that are currently unavailable (accept UAC).
  • Clear Turn on fast startup → Save changes.

Disable the built-in battery

  • Restart and press F1 at the logo to enter ThinkPad Setup.
  • Config → Power → Disable built-in battery, Enter, then Yes.
  • The laptop shuts off. Wait 3–5 minutes.
Do not skip this. Otherwise the battery stays live while you work on the board.

2Open it up

Manual p. 72 (base cover), p. 76 (battery)

Remove the base cover

Lay the laptop upside down with the hinges away from you. Loosen the 5 captive screws (step 1), then lift the cover up from the edges near the hinges (arrows 2).

Unplug the battery

Peel back the tape flap (1), then pull the battery connector straight off its socket (2). The connector is in the upper-middle of the battery edge.

Remove the battery

Remove 6 screws, then lift the battery out (4).

3Install the antennas

Manual p. 83–84. The manual shows removal. Installation is the same steps in reverse.

Read before you start. The manual's figure below is labeled for removal, in the order 1 → 5. To install, work 5 → 1 (seat the antennas, then route, screw down, connect).

Study the figure

The M1.6 screws are the small ones. Keep them apart from the M2s.

Seat the two antenna modules and route the cables

One antenna module sits at the upper-left edge and the other at the lower-right, near the ports. Set each into its pocket (step 5 in the figure), then run the cable along the front edge through the guides.

No tension. Cables must not be pulled tight. Tension can damage them against the cable guides or break a wire. Press each cable fully into its guides.

Fasten the antenna screws

Install the 4 × M2 × L3.2 (step 1) and 3 × M1.6 × L2.6 (step 2) screws where the figure shows them. Snug only. Torque is 0.181 Nm, which is very light.

No screw holes? On a unit built without WWAN the chassis may lack these screw bosses, along with the SIM tray. Don't force screws or drill. See the project README for what we found.

Connect the multi-pin connector (step 4)

Plug in the connector shown as step 4 on p. 83. The manual says the antenna assembly comes with the P-sensor cable, which goes to the P-sensor connector on the board.

4Install the cellular modem

Manual p. 74. Again shown as removal, so reverse it.

Seat the card

The WWAN slot is near the right end, beside the SSD area. Push the card into the M.2 slot (reverse of removal step 3), lay it flat over the standoff, and fasten one screw.

ScrewsTypeColorTorque
1M2 × L2.2 mm, big-hatBlack0.181 Nm (1.85 kgf-cm)

Plug in the two antenna cables

Orange cable → main connector. Blue cable → auxiliary connector. This is the manual's own rule for installing.

Press each connector straight down until it clicks. Do not angle it.

5Close up

Reverse the order of section 2.

Reinstall the battery

Set it in place, install the 6 × M2 × L4.5 mm silver screws, plug the battery connector back in, and lay the tape flap down.

Check the cables

Before the cover goes on, look for any antenna cable that is pinched, lifted out of a guide, or lying across a screw hole.

Refit the base cover and SIM

Snap the cover down along all edges, tighten the 5 captive screws, then insert the nano-SIM back into its tray.

6Verify

Not from the manual. General steps. general

Power on

Plug in the AC adapter. On ThinkPads the disabled built-in battery re-enables once AC power is connected, which may take a moment before the machine will start.

Check that Windows sees the modem

  • Device Manager → Network adapters / Modems for the WWAN device.
  • Settings → Network & Internet → Cellular. Install the modem vendor's driver if it is missing.
  • Check ThinkPad Setup (F1) → Config → Network for any WWAN setting that is turned off.

If the card is not detected, power down and reseat it. If Setup shows a "unsupported" error, the card is not on Lenovo's allowed list for this machine.
